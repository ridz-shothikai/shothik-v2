New Campaign Setup Guide (All-Funnel in One Ad Set)
1. Campaign Structure
Campaign Objective: Conversions (Sales or Leads, depending on your business).


Number of Campaigns: 1 per goal.


Ad Set: Just one broad ad set — targeting cold + warm users together.


Use Advantage+ (broad targeting).


Only restrict on things like location, age, or language if necessary.


Ads: 10–50 ads inside that ad set.


👉 Meta will automatically use different ads as Top of Funnel (TOF), Middle of Funnel (MOF), and Bottom of Funnel (BOF) depending on each user’s stage of awareness.

🎨 Creative Strategy (The Core)
Meta now decides who sees what, so your creative library becomes the targeting mechanism. The goal is to create ads that map to different user personas and different awareness stages.
2. Awareness Ladder (stages of user journey)
Problem Aware:


Audience: People who don’t even realize the problem yet.


Creative: Educational, thought-provoking ads.


Example: “Did you know most small businesses lose 30% of sales because of XYZ problem?”


Solution Aware:


Audience: People know the problem but not the solution.


Creative: Demonstrate how the problem can be solved.


Example: Explainer videos, carousel ads showing “3 ways to fix XYZ.”


Product Aware:


Audience: People know there are solutions but not your product.


Creative: Product demo, feature highlight, side-by-side comparison.


Most Aware / Conversion Ready:


Audience: People ready to buy.


Creative: Testimonials, case studies, strong CTA (“Sign up today / Buy now”).



🧑 User Personas → Creative Mapping
When analyzing a business, identify who the real buyers are and what pain points they care about. For each persona, design creatives across the funnel stages.
Example (for an e-commerce fitness brand):
Persona A: Busy Professionals


TOF Ad: “Struggling to stay fit while working long hours?” (Problem-aware video).


MOF Ad: “Quick 20-minute workout you can do anywhere” (Solution explainer).


BOF Ad: “See how 2,000 professionals lost weight with our plan” (Testimonial + CTA).


Persona B: New Moms


TOF Ad: “Did you know post-pregnancy weight can affect energy?” (Awareness).


MOF Ad: “Safe 10-minute daily exercises” (How-to demo).


BOF Ad: “Hear Sarah’s story: lost 10 lbs with us in 6 weeks” (UGC testimonial).


Persona C: Fitness Enthusiasts


TOF Ad: “Why most workouts plateau after 6 weeks” (Problem).


MOF Ad: “Upgrade your workout with resistance tracking” (Product feature).


BOF Ad: “Claim your free trial today” (CTA heavy).



📐 Formats to Include
Meta explicitly wants format diversity:
Short video (Reels/TikTok-style) → fast, relatable, UGC look.


Longer video (60–90s) → demos, founder stories, explainer.


Static images → product shots, bold copy, simple offers.


Carousel ads → problem → solution → testimonial flow.


Story format ads → immersive, CTA swipe-up.



⚙️ Optimization Process
Launch 10–50 creatives across personas + awareness stages.


Don’t judge purely by ROAS. Meta may spend on a “bad” ROAS ad because it’s being used as TOF.


Kill the non-spenders → The real underperformers are ads that get no budget at all.


Refresh monthly → Add 5–10 new creatives (new angles + refreshed versions of winners).


Watch budget flow → If Meta shifts spend from one TOF ad to another, that’s your new top-funnel winner.



✅ Summary
One campaign, one broad ad set, many creatives.


Creative diversity = the new targeting.


Map ads to personas + awareness stages.


Use all formats (video, static, carousel, story).


Optimize by pruning non-spenders and refreshing monthly.
