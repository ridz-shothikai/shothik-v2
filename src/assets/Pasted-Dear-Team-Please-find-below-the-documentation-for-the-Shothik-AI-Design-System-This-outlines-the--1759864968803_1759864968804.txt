**Shothik AI Design System Documentation**

**Brand Philosophy**

The Shothik AI design system embraces Steve Jobs' minimalism principles, emphasizing that "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." We believe every element should serve a distinct purpose, achieved through radical focus and the strategic use of intentional white space.

**Color Palette**

Our color palette is carefully curated to reflect our brand identity and enhance usability.

**Primary Brand Colors**

* **Primary Brand:** #005249 (HSL: 180, 100%, 15%) - A deep teal representing trust, stability, and growth. This color is primarily used for key Calls to Action (CTAs) and core brand elements.
* **Secondary Brand:** #00AA93 (HSL: 174, 100%, 33%) - A vibrant teal that embodies innovation and energy. This is used for interactive elements and visual highlights.

**Neutral Foundation**

* **Pure White:** #FFFFFF - The primary background color, inspired by a Jobs-ian "clean slate" to ensure clarity and focus.
* **Deep Black:** #000000 - Used for essential typography and primary CTAs, providing strong visual hierarchy.
* **Charcoal:** #374151 (HSL: 215, 25%, 27%) - Applied to body text and secondary content for comfortable readability.
* **Light Gray:** #6B7280 (HSL: 215, 16%, 47%) - Used for muted text and labels, providing subtle visual cues.

**Accent Colors**

* **Accent Light:** #EAFDF9 (HSL: 160, 60%, 96%) - Used for subtle background variations and hover states, adding gentle visual interest.
* **Accent Highlight:** #E7FEFE (HSL: 180, 100%, 96%) - Provides visual feedback for interactive elements.

**Trust & Security Indicators**

* **Trust Green:** #22C55E (HSL: 142, 71%, 45%) - Indicates success states and is used for security badges, fostering user confidence.
* **Security Blue:** #3B82F6 (HSL: 213, 94%, 68%) - Used for general trust indicators and certifications.
* **Warning Amber:** #F59E0B (HSL: 38, 92%, 50%) - Signals caution and draws attention to important states.

**Typography System**

Our typography system is built for clarity and visual appeal, ensuring a consistent reading experience.

**Font Stack**

* **Primary:** 'Inter' - A clean and highly readable sans-serif font intended for body text.
* **Display:** 'Manrope' - A geometric sans-serif font for impactful headlines and hero text.
* **Fallback:** system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif - Ensures a reliable rendering across different systems.

**Typography Hierarchy**

* **Hero Headlines:** 7xl-9xl (112px-224px), font-thin, tracking-tight - For prominent headings on landing pages and key sections.
* **Section Headlines:** 4xl-6xl (36px-60px), font-bold, letter-spacing -0.025em - For clear demarcation of content sections.
* **Body Large:** 3xl (30px), font-light - Often used for subtext within hero sections, providing additional context.
* **Body Regular:** Base (16px), line-height 1.6 - The standard font size and line height for optimal readability of paragraph text.
* **Small Text:** sm (14px) - Used for captions, metadata, and less prominent information.

**Font Features**

We utilize specific font features to enhance typography:

* `font-feature-settings: 'cv02', 'cv03', 'cv04', 'cv11';` - Enables stylistic alternates for improved visual harmony.
* `text-rendering: optimizeLegibility;` - Optimizes text rendering for better readability.
* `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased;` - Improves the appearance of text on web browsers.

**Layout & Spacing**

Consistent layout and spacing are crucial for a clean and organized interface.

**Container System**

* **Max Width: 5xl (1024px)** - Used for hero sections to maintain focus and readability.
* **Max Width: 7xl (1280px)** - Applied to content sections to provide ample space while maintaining structure.
* **Responsive Padding:** `px-4` (mobile) → `px-6` (tablet) → `px-8` (desktop) - Ensures consistent horizontal padding across different screen sizes.

**Spacing Scale (Tailwind)**

We utilize a Tailwind-inspired spacing scale for consistent application of margins and padding:

* **Micro: 1-2 (4px-8px)** - Primarily used for spacing within icons and for borders.
* **Small: 4-6 (16px-24px)** - For internal spacing within components.
* **Medium: 8-12 (32px-48px)** - Applied for spacing between different sections of content.
* **Large: 16-20 (64px-80px)** - Used for significant gaps between major sections.
* **Extra Large: 24-32 (96px-128px)** - Typically used for spacing in hero sections to create visual breathing room.

**Interactive Elements**

Our interactive elements are designed to be intuitive and provide clear feedback.

**Button Styles**

* **Primary CTA:**
* Background: Pure black (#000000)
* Text: White
* Padding: `px-20 py-6` (80px horizontal, 24px vertical)
* Border-radius: `xl` (12px)
* Font: `2xl` (24px), font-light
* Hover: Scale 1.02, `bg-gray-900` - Provides a clear and engaging call to action.
* **Secondary Button:**
* Background: Transparent
* Border: 1px solid border color (context-dependent)
* Text: Foreground color (context-dependent)
* Hover: Background fill with a subtle color - Offers a less prominent action.

**Micro-Interactions**

Subtle animations and transitions enhance the user experience:

* **Scale:** 1.01-1.02 for gentle hover effects on interactive elements.
* **Duration:** 300-500ms for smooth and natural transitions.
* **Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)` for a natural and engaging motion curve.

**Component Design Principles**

Our components are designed with reusability and consistency in mind.

**Cards & Containers**

* Background: Pure white (#FFFFFF) with subtle shadows for visual lift.
* Border: 1px solid `slate-200/50` for clear definition.
* Border-radius: `2xl` (16px) for a modern aesthetic.
* Shadow: Soft, minimal shadows to provide depth without being distracting.

**Form Elements**

* Input Height: Minimum 44px to ensure comfortable touch interaction.
* Focus States: `ring-2` with the primary brand color at 20% opacity for clear visual feedback.
* Placeholder: Muted foreground color for subtle guidance.
* Error States: Utilize the warning amber color with clear and concise error messaging.

**Responsive Design**

We adopt a mobile-first approach to ensure our designs are accessible and performant across all devices.

**Breakpoint Strategy**

* **Mobile First Approach:** Designs are initially optimized for mobile devices and then progressively enhanced for larger screens.
* **Breakpoints:**
* `Base`: 0-640px (mobile)
* `sm`: 640px+ (large mobile)
* `md`: 768px+ (tablet)
* `lg`: 1024px+ (desktop)
* `xl`: 1280px+ (large desktop)

* **Touch Targets:** Interactive elements have a minimum size of 44px × 44px for easy interaction on touchscreens.
